- H1: Real pharmacies. / Real results.

**Case study cards**

1. **MapleMeds Pharmacies** · Ontario · Clinical and Vaccinations
   - Title: MapleMeds Pharmacies took clinical and vaccination revenue to record levels
   - Summary: MapleMeds runs its minor ailments and immunization programs on ZeeNovo Clinical, using one-click vaccine auto-fill and automated dose reminders across its stores.
   - Result: Record clinical and vaccination revenue

2. **Oshawa Lakeview Pharmacy** · Ontario · Clinical and Website
   - Title: Oshawa Lakeview Pharmacy grew clinical revenue and simplified daily operations
   - Summary: After moving to ZeeNovo Clinical and launching its ZeeNovo website, the pharmacy improved clinical revenue and made daily clinical work easier to run, from guided assessments to automatic documentation and fax.
   - Result: Simpler daily clinical operations

3. **KW Bruce Pharmacies** · Ontario · New store launch
   - Title: KW Bruce Pharmacies opened a new store with a head start
   - Summary: KW Bruce built its new location on ZeeNovo from day one, using online and voice booking to bring in patients from the first week.
   - Result: Early patient traction from day one
