**FAQ** (needs design approval)
- Do I need an account to book? No. You can book on your pharmacy's website. An account lets you manage bookings and requests in one place.
- Is there a cost to use the portal? No. The pharmacy may charge for some services, as it would in person.
- Can I book for my family? Yes. Group booking lets you book several people in one appointment.
